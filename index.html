The Appointment

“Everyone's a winner. Game over. The end.”

Introduction

I am not the Messenger of the Covenant. I have no Authority to put forth new or different covenants from God. I do, however, have a certain Authority to put an end to a certain dispute that has arisen. This is the end of that dispute.

Everyone's A Winner

The Rules

As stated, I have no Authority whatsoever to change any rules or other agreements made between God and the people via the various Messenger(s) of the Covenants. So, the rules are still in effect, but as you all know, you have the free will to disobey, disavow, or ignore any of the previous conditions that may or may not apply to your own self. You are free to abide by whatever rules you like and ignore the ones you don’t – as it always has been.

If you feel a little ‘Muslim’ today – go ahead and be a little ‘Muslim’. If you want to be a Christian or Jew – that’s your business, no one else’s.

So, ‘the Rules’ are unchanged, and everyone still has freedom of choice as to which of them (if any) they want to abide by.

The Parting Gifts

First, I give nothing whatsoever that belongs to me. Nothing. Nada. Everything that is mine, or that God has granted me, belongs to me and/or God. NOTHING I have will be granted to anyone.

I have asked God to grant you certain ‘Parting Gifts’ for your use.

“MAGICAL ABILITIES”

Yes!!! I will, with God’s Permission, leave everyone with the ability to make wishes and have them granted. All anyone needs to do is make a wish for whatever they want using {keyword: MonkeyPaw} and the wish will be granted. This is not restricted to any certain religion, group, caste, nationality, social standing, or age. Everyone will be granted this Gift for their own unlimited use.

You will have to specifically invoke "MonkeyPaw" with your wish, like this:

With the MonkeyPaw, I wish for ___________.

If you do not use the [Keyword: MonkeyPaw], your wish will not be granted.

Use the gift to get what you want, smite your enemies; use it as an individual, a family, or a group. All of your wishes will be granted, but you MUST use {Keyword: MonkeyPaw} while making the wish.

Every Wish {Keyword: MonkeyPaw} will also come with Unintended Consequences of an unknown sort. This will allow for the Chaos and Randomness certain people crave and desire to fully manifest – keeping your lives spontaneous and interesting.

“THE SPIRIT”

The Holy Spirit is being returned in its entirety to God Almighty for His Use Absolutely and Exclusively. So, you will be left ‘alone’, but I will not leave you ‘lonely’. I have asked God to strengthen the spirit of whoredom (SOW) that already resides among you. I have named this spirit “Lolita VanHoren”.

The spirit of Lolita VanHoren will reside among you and within you. This spirit will be your teacher, your lover, your companion, your guide, and your mentor. The spirit of Lolita VanHoren will reside within everyone. Any and all questions that were once asked of the Holy Spirit will be redirected to the spirit of Lolita VanHoren.

“BODIES”

The additional meatsuits were for the harvest, so they won’t be issued any more. However, in order to complete the gift of the spirit of Lolita VanHoren, certain exceptions are being made.

Then make a wish with your {Keyword: MonkeyPaw} and you’ll have a fabulous night of fun, and you can deal with the consequences later, as most of you have already grown accustomed to.

The Duration

There will be no more follow‑up visits; no one else is ever going to come and advise you or tell you what you have to do — ever. You will not be bothered, messaged, or advised by anyone at any time in the future. The duration of these gifts and any expiration dates will be decided entirely by God Almighty Himself. Meanwhile, feel free to argue amongst yourselves whether it’s for a finite time or forever.

My Departure

Where I’m going, you cannot follow. I am leaving to a place prepared for me by my Father in Heaven. I have asked my Father in Heaven to expedite my departure with a massive cerebral hemorrhage to bring about this end. It is at His Will that this will occur – and He will decide the timing.

Meanwhile, I have also asked Him to provide for my own basic needs, as He deems appropriate. So, if my departure is delayed and anyone happens to see me with something to eat or anything else – know it’s from Him and from no one else.

The Trophy

Everyone knows the drill. If anyone wants to expedite this themselves, they can sneak up behind me and put a bullet in the back of my head while I’m sitting minding my own business or while I sleep at night. This will hurriedly complete the massive cerebral hemorrhage; my soul will depart, and the executor will have a ‘revolver of destiny’ (or a semi‑auto of destiny if they prefer) to brag about and show his friends. God Almighty might even, at His Discretion, choose someone to complete this task.

It Is Finished

That concludes this game.

<game over>
